🏛️ Lok Sabha — Deep Dive
❓

Why is it held?

To elect 543 Members of Parliament (MPs) who form the national government and pass laws for the entire country.

📅

When is it conducted?

Every 5 years, typically between April–May. The last election was in 2024; the next is expected in 2029.

💺

How many seats?

543 constituencies across all states and Union Territories. Each constituency elects one MP. 2 Anglo-Indian seats were removed in 2020.

🏢

Major Parties

BJP, INC, AAP, TMC, DMK, SP, BSP, NCP, JD(U), Shiv Sena, and many regional parties — over 2,500+ registered parties nationwide.

👔

Key Positions Elected

Prime Minister (leader of majority party/coalition), Speaker of Lok Sabha, and Leader of Opposition.

🗳️

Who can vote?

Any Indian citizen aged 18+ registered in the electoral roll. Voting is done using EVMs with VVPAT verification.

🏢 Vidhan Sabha — Deep Dive
❓

Why is it held?

To elect Members of Legislative Assembly (MLAs) who form the state government, pass state laws, and manage state budgets.

📅

When is it conducted?

Every 5 years for each state, on different schedules. States like UP, Maharashtra, Bihar hold elections at different times.

💺

How many seats?

Varies by state — UP has 403 seats (largest), Sikkim has 32 seats (smallest). Total ~4,100+ MLA seats across India.

🏢

Major Parties

National parties plus strong regional parties like TRS (Telangana), AIADMK (TN), JMM (Jharkhand), BJD (Odisha), and many more.

👔

Key Positions Elected

Chief Minister (leader of majority party), Speaker of Vidhan Sabha, Deputy CM, and Cabinet Ministers.

📋

What does it control?

State police, healthcare, agriculture, state roads, education, and state-level taxes like GST share and stamp duty.

🏘️ Local Body — Deep Dive
❓

Why is it held?

To elect local representatives who manage civic services — water, sanitation, street lights, and local roads in your area.

📅

When is it conducted?

Every 5 years, scheduled by the State Election Commission. Timing varies widely across states and municipalities.

💺

Types of Bodies

Municipal Corporation (large cities), Municipality/Nagar Palika (towns), Gram Panchayat (villages). India has 2.5 lakh+ panchayats.

🏢

Parties & Independents

Local elections often see strong independent candidates. Party influence varies — national parties compete in cities, locals dominate villages.

👔

Key Positions Elected

Mayor / Sarpanch, Councillors / Ward Members, Block Pramukh, and Zila Parishad Members.

🌟

Special Feature

Local elections have reserved seats for SC/ST/OBC and women (33-50% reservation) — ensuring grassroots representation.
