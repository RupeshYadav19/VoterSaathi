How to use cVIGIL App 🚨

cVIGIL is a user-friendly app developed by the Election Commission of India to report any violation of the Model Code of Conduct. Here is how you can get started:

Step 1

Download & Install

Search for "cVIGIL" on Play Store or App Store. Download the official ECI app.

Android 🤖 iOS 🍎
Step 2

Capture Violation

Open the app and click a photo or record a video. It automatically tags your GPS location.

Step 3

Action in 100 Mins

The Field Squad reaches the spot within 15 mins. Final decision made in 100 mins!

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find my Polling Booth? 🏫

You can find your booth by entering your EPIC number on the Electoral Search portal.

Can I vote without a Voter ID card? 💳

Yes! If your name is in the voter list, you can carry any of the 12 alternative IDs like Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport.
