Register

How to register for a Voter ID card? It's simple!

Journey Board 🗺️
1

Visit Official Portal

Go to the official portal at voters.eci.gov.in or use the app.

2

Sign Up / Login

Create an account with your mobile number to get started.

3

Select Form 6

Choose "New Voter Registration" to add your name to the list.

4

Fill Your Details

Enter personal info and address accurately from your documents.

5

Upload Proofs

Upload your photo, age proof, and address proof copies.

6

Submit & Track

Review, submit, and save your Reference ID for tracking.

cake Am I 18+?

Enter your date of birth to check if you're eligible!
