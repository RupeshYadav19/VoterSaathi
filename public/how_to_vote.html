Voting Steps 📋

Step 1: Enter Booth

Go to your assigned polling booth with a valid ID. The officer will verify your name and mark your finger with indelible ink.

Step 2: Press the Button

Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate. A red light glows and you'll hear a long beep confirming your vote.

Step 3: Check VVPAT

A printed slip appears in the VVPAT window for 7 seconds showing who you voted for. Verify it before it drops into the sealed box.

Step 4: Done!

Your vote is securely recorded. Leave the booth quietly. Your vote is secret — nobody can see who you voted for!

What is VVPAT?

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail prints a slip with the candidate name & symbol for 7 seconds so you can verify your choice before it drops into a sealed box.

What is NOTA?

None Of The Above allows voters to officially reject all candidates. Introduced in 2013 by the Supreme Court of India.
